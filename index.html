Technology

Three steps.
Two metals.
No furnace.

Electricity does the separating. Each metal plates out at its own potential.

STEP 1

Liberate

Cells and ribbons are separated from glass, frame and polymer.

STEP 2

Dissolve

A low-temperature leach takes silver and copper into solution.

STEP 3

Plate

Controlled-potential electrowinning recovers silver first, then copper.
